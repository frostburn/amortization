## What the attempts show

- Mission 01 takes two complementary wins: armed extraction with the evidence
  and a quiet, nonlethal rescue. Together they cover every medal.
- Mission 02's quiet SHUNT approach succeeds without firing. No disguise and
  Open channel are earned in separate full-crew wins. Five failures and four
  abandoned attempts show the challenge of those constraints; no difficulty
  reduction is needed to make the complete medal set attainable.
- `501e9cf9` fires 39 shots without an alarm. That still qualifies for Low
  profile: its rule is no site alarm, not no shots. Local combat and alarm are
  deliberately distinct. `b6195d77` earns Open channel while killing one guard,
  so it correctly does not receive Nonlethal.

## Extraction route correction

In `7be4687d`, the VAN order at tick 1120 sends the wounded carrier and the
SHUNT operator from the public west street back through the hostile compound.
A public route exists around the south wall. Mission 02 now uses the same
perimeter preference as Mission 10 when both ends of an extraction order are
outside the restricted area. Ordinary ground orders remain explicit routes.

The regression checks the actual issued paths and isolates physical route
completion from enemy fire. The carrier has 20 HP at this point; simply letting
the revised order run still loses Morrow to pursuit fire. This is a route fix,
not a promise of rescuing an already damaged attempt without intervention.

All seven supplied wins retain their outcome, survivor count and medals under
the revised mission configuration. One old failure, `ffb8084d`, now still has
one survivor at the recording's cutoff, so its old loss is no longer an expected
result. Archive recordings require **Try current rules** after this map-config
change; their original JSON/checkpoints are never rewritten to claim a match.

## The apparent archive soft-lock

In `0295743a`, two survivors are inside the archive after their SHUNT operator
dies. CUT already supports the inside face of the shutter. A free-handed
operative can reach it and breach the lock; the recorded wounded crew still
faces the guards outside. The problem is discoverability: CUT's map marker is
outside, and the status only says the shutter is locked.

A visible closed-shutter panel now explains that CUT works from inside and
provides **Send selected to CUT · 8s**. The existing free-hands requirement and
noisy eight-second breach remain. Using it sacrifices Light touch as before.

## Results and replayability

A persistent MISSION COMPLETE / MISSION FAILED map splash appears immediately.
The result dialog waits until the van has departed on victory, or three seconds
on defeat. View results becomes available after that delay; dismissing the
dialog returns to the splash with restart and (on wins) next-operation controls.
Survivors and the friendly witness board the selected extraction van and drive
off; surviving guards continue their patrols after either outcome. The finished
simulation remains frozen for medals, times, recorded checkpoints and exports.
The presentation runs on a separate copy and pauses behind dialogs or when the
page is hidden. Watching a replay preserves its recorded final scene.

Restart mission is present in briefings, Operations and result dialogs, as well
as the map's existing Restart control. Shift+R also works in mission dialogs;
notes and replay tools retain their own input handling. Restarting preserves
previously earned medals and archives the incomplete attempt in dev playtesting.
